About

Research at the intersection of reasoning, agents, and biomedicine.

I am a master's student in computer science at the University of Science and Technology of China. My work focuses on building AI systems that can reason over multiple sources of evidence, coordinate specialized agents, and operate reliably in knowledge-intensive settings.

I am especially interested in multi-hop reasoning, retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge graph integration, clinical decision support, and autonomous scientific experimentation.

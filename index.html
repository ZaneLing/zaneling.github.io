About

I am a master's student in computer science at the University of Science and Technology of China. My research focuses on reliable reasoning over complex evidence, collaboration among specialized AI agents, and knowledge-intensive biomedical applications.

My current interests include multi-hop question answering, retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge graphs, clinical decision support, and autonomous biological experimentation.

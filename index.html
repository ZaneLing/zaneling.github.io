Hi there! I am a master's student in Computer Science at the University of Science and Technology of China. My research focuses on language model reasoning and multi-agent systems, especially for biomedical and clinical applications.

I am interested in how agents retrieve evidence, coordinate specialized roles, and make more reliable decisions. I am currently an intern at ByteDance.

I am seeking PhD opportunities in North America in medical AI, clinical NLP, and AI for healthcare.

Ziyang Ling, Ronald X. Xu, Mingzhai Sun

Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL) 2026

A brain-inspired framework that separates global planning from local evidence retrieval, with verification and repair to improve multi-hop question answering.

From Hindsight to Foresight: Dissecting Clinical Evidence Acquisition in Large Language Models

Ziyang Ling

Preprint · arXiv, 2026

Beyond Reachability: A Relation-Semantic Reasoning Framework for Biomedical Multi-hop QA

Ziyang Ling, Ziyi Chen, Ronald X. Xu, Mingzhai Sun

Preprint · arXiv, 2026

ReSemReason uses question-specific relation schemas to find biomedical answers with valid supporting paths, rather than relying on graph reachability alone.

BioPRM: Step-Level Consistency Verification of Biological Protocols

Bowen Shao, Ziyang Ling, Ronald Xu, Mingzhai Sun

Preprint · arXiv, 2026

A contextual process reward model that checks biological protocols step by step for inconsistencies with the experimental goal and surrounding procedure.

FGSS-BO: Feedback-Guided Hypothesis Management for LLM-Based Bayesian Optimization

Hao Chen, Ziyang Ling, Ronald X. Xu, Mingzhai Sun

Preprint · arXiv, 2026

FGSS-BO links search hypotheses to evidence and test outcomes, helping an LLM-guided Bayesian optimizer revise its choices across rounds.

Training-Free BioNEL-RAG: Advancing Biomedical Entity Linking through Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Large Language Models

Ziyi Chen, Hui Liu, Ziyang Ling, Ronald X. Xu, Mingzhai Sun

2025 6th International Conference on Intelligent Computing and Human-Computer Interaction (ICHCI), pp. 304–308

A training-free method that retrieves biomedical entity candidates and uses a language model to select the best match from context.

RAMP: Researcher-Agent Modeling Pipeline for Retinal Organoid Morphogenesis

Junao Song, Yi Wang, Yilin Qiao, Haoran Zhang, Mohan Yang, Fenghao Gu, Wenteng Gao, Ziyang Ling, Ronald Xu, Mingzhai Sun

Submitted to PNAS · 2026
